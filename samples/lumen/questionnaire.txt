VENDOR SECURITY QUESTIONNAIRE - Lumen Type Foundry
Completed by: Tomas Lindqvist, Licensing (tomas@lumentype.example)

Q1. Will you receive or store any of our data? A1. No.
Q2. Do you install software on our systems? A2. Only font files, copied into the user's font folder by an optional helper script.
Q3. Subprocessors? A3. None; we receive no customer data.
Q4. Security attestations? A4. None; not applicable for a font licence.
